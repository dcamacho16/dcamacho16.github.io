Hi, I'm **Daniel** — a third-year Telecommunications Engineering student at the [Universidad Católica de Murcia (UCAM)](https://www.ucam.edu/) with a double-major background in **Mathematics and Physics** from Simpson College (Iowa, USA).

Over the last few years I've worked as a **data analyst and operations engineer** across companies in Spain and the United States — building ETL pipelines with SSIS, designing Power BI dashboards for retail networks, and applying predictive analytics to optimize critical infrastructure. I'm particularly drawn to the intersection of **telecommunications, data engineering, and digital infrastructure**: turning raw signals into systems people can actually use.

My toolkit includes **Python, SQL, Power BI, MATLAB/Simulink, SSIS/ETL, Power Automate, and REST APIs**, and I work fluently in **Spanish (native), English (C2)** — built up over nearly a decade living in the US — and **French (B2)**.

Outside of engineering I enjoy languages and traveling. Have a look at my [CV](/cv/) for the full story, or feel free to [reach out](mailto:camacho.daniel11@gmail.com).
